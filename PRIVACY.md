# Privacy Policy — reel-autopilot

Last updated: November 10, 2026

**reel-autopilot** is a personal automation tool used only by its owner.
It helps the owner manage their own YouTube channel: it downloads videos
the owner chooses, adds a small border and channel name, and uploads them
to the owner's own YouTube channel at scheduled times.

## What data the app can touch

- **YouTube**: upload videos to the owner's channel and read view counts
  of videos it uploaded. Nothing else on the channel.
- **Google Drive**: only files the app itself created (temporary video
  files). It cannot see, read, or delete any other files in the Drive.
- No other Google data is accessed.

## Where data is stored

- Video links and settings are stored in the owner's own GitHub repository.
- Sign-in keys (OAuth tokens) are stored in GitHub Secrets — visible to nobody.
- No data is sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
- No analytics. No tracking. No advertising.
